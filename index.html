I am a second-year master's student at KAIST School of Computing, co-advised by Prof. Joseph Seering and Prof. Juho Kim. Currently, I'm part of both CSTL and KIXLAB.

I design and study conversational AI systems that support learners' reasoning, reflection, and agency, as well as teachers' pedagogical decisions. My research draws on Human-Computer Interaction and Learning Sciences, with an emphasis on learners' critical engagement with AI.

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

Ongoing Work

coRead: Linked Reasoning Steps to Guide Critical Reading Discussions

A system in which two LLM peer agents guide critical reading discussions of research papers. The agents track each learner's progress through linked reasoning steps and adapt their support.

Textbook Reading Discussions with Two AI Agents in Middle School English Classes

With a middle school English teacher, we are building an environment where students read their textbook and discuss it with two AI agents.
